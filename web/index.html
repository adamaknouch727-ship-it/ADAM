How to read this tool

  • Niche score (0–100) — the headline. It blends demand (42%), the inverse of competition (38%) and profitability (20%). 72+ is a goldmine, under 36 is best avoided.
  • Demand — how much money page one actually moves: estimated royalties, median sales per book, and how often Amazon's autocomplete suggests the phrase.
  • Competition — lower is better. Built from the review wall on page one, how many competing books exist, how few weak (<50 review) books there are, and how many big publishers hold slots.
  • Profitability — price level and royalty per sale after KDP's 60% print / 70% Kindle split and printing costs.
  • Royalties / mo — sum of estimated monthly royalties for the analysed books on page one. It is the size of the prize, not your share of it.
  • Sales estimates come from a Best Sellers Rank → sales curve, scaled per marketplace. Without deep scan the rank is inferred from search position and review count; turn on Deep scan for real BSRs.

A workflow that works

  1. Search a broad seed, sort by niche score.
  2. Shortlist anything scoring 60+ with median reviews under ~150.
  3. Open the drawer and read page one: if half the books look sloppy, you can beat them.
  4. Re-run the winner as its own seed with Wide depth to find sub-niches.
  5. Export the CSV and plan your covers and interiors.

Estimates are directional, not accounting. Amazon publishes no sales data; every KDP tool models it.
